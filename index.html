About Me

Hello! Nice to meet you, I'm Sean. I am a Singer, Actor, Dancer, Musician and proud Japanese-American, Southern California native. When I was a little kid, I was always mesmerized with entertainment. I would constantly watch our family's discs of classics like The Producers, Aladdin, and Spiderman (Toby Mcguire version of course). My early love for movies, quickly translated to an enamorment with live performance. Wicked, Matilda, and Miss Saigon are just a few of the many shows that made me fall in love with theatre- and decide to pursue it professionally. Storytelling is one of the most ancient, sacred, and powerful practices of humanity. I am so lucky that I get to create art. Some of my favorite hobbies are Basketball, Hiking, and Board Games!
